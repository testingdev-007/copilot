From summary to systemWorkshop page · Step 2

Three prompts, in this order

A · Clean
Make all dates DD/MM/YYYY, scores whole numbers 1-5, remove duplicate rows, flag blank comments. Tell me what you changed.
B · Find themes
Add a Theme column (5 to 6 themes). Show counts and average score, most mentioned first.
C · Three audiences
Write: a 3-sentence exec note, a bulleted action list for the project team, and a friendly "You said, we're doing" for all staff. No jargon.

From summary to systemWorkshop page · Step 4

The Idea Box in three prompts

1 Build me a single-file HTML page where colleagues can submit ideas for improving how we welcome new starters. One file, no internet needed, friendly.
2 Add a category dropdown, let people upvote ideas, and sort the most popular to the top.
3 Add a bar chart of ideas per category and a button to download all ideas as a CSV. Add three example ideas.

From summary to systemWorkshop page · Step 4

Five rules of thumb

  • One thing at a time. Small requests, small surprises.
  • Test after every change. Click everything.
  • Describe what you see. "The button does nothing" beats guessing why.
  • Tangled? Start fresh. New chat, paste the working file, say what to change.
  • Before sharing, ask: "Does this send any data anywhere?"

From summary to systemWorkshop page · Step 5

Before you share it

Green: go

  • Public information
  • Your own draft writing
  • Made-up example data

Amber: check first

  • Internal policies
  • Anonymous survey results
  • Client material

Red: ask first

  • Personal details
  • Health, pay, legal
  • Passwords and keys

And for anything you share: a named owner, a tested example, a review date.
